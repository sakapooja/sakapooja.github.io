About me

I'm Pooja Saka, an Information Technology undergraduate at Vidya Jyothi Institute of Technology (2024–2027) with a CGPA of 8.66/10, after completing a Diploma in Civil Engineering at Govt. Polytechnic Masab Tank. I'm working toward a career as a software developer, and I enjoy turning ideas into working applications that are reliable, interactive and easy to use.

I program in Java, Python and SQL, and build for the web with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js, Node.js and REST APIs, backed by a foundation in OOP, DBMS, data structures, operating systems and computer networks. I've built an AI-powered legal compliance system using LLMs and retrieval-augmented generation, a wearable smart pendant for real-time health monitoring, an NLP model that detects harmful speech, and an IoT child-safety monitor. I've also completed virtual internships with Deloitte, ServiceNow and EduSkills.

I'm looking for an entry-level opportunity where I can apply my technical skills, contribute to real-world projects and keep growing as a software developer.
